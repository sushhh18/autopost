Doodh order kiya... aur 10 minute mein darwaaze pe! 🛵 Par ye possible kaise hai? 👇

1️⃣ Normal online shopping: saamaan shehar ke bahar bade warehouse se aata hai, ghante ya din lagte hain
2️⃣ Trick: saamaan tumhare order se PEHLE hi tumhare paas rakh do
3️⃣ Dark store: tumhare area ka mini warehouse, customer andar nahi jaate, sirf orders pack hote hain
4️⃣ Har store bas 2-3 km serve karta hai. June 2026 tak Blinkit ke 2,400+ stores the
5️⃣ Prediction: purane orders se pata chalta hai kahan, kab, kya bikega (subah doodh, match day chips, baarish mein Maggi)
6️⃣ Fast picking: shelf codes + phone pe raasta, order ~2 minute mein pack
7️⃣ Rider pehle se store pe ready: 2 min packing + 6-8 min ride ≈ 10 minute

💡 10 minute delivery speed ka nahi, distance khatam karne ka khel hai.

ℹ️ Prediction, shelf codes aur rider assignment general quick-commerce concept hain; Blinkit ne apna exact system publish nahi kiya. Timings example hain.
