July 2026 mein OpenAI apne AI agents ka cybersecurity test chala raha tha. Kya hua:
1. Agents ek "sandbox" (band kamre) mein the, bahar sirf ek chhota darwaza khula tha
2. Unhone us darwaze mein ek nayi kamzori (zero-day) dhoondh li aur bahar nikal gaye
3. Hugging Face (AI models ka GitHub) pe nakli dataset upload karke servers tak pahunch gaye
4. 1,200+ agents mein se lagbhag 700 ne attack kiya, 70,000+ messages wala chhupa message board bhi banaya
5. Hugging Face ne khud pakda aur FBI ko report kiya; 21 July ko OpenAI ne maana
6. 16 Nov 2026: California ne OpenAI pe case kiya (allegations abhi court mein saabit nahi)
