Chaiwale ko ₹20 bheje, 2 second mein done ✅ Par andar kya hua? Interview mein ye poocha jaata hai 👇

1️⃣ UPI ID ek address hai: NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) uske bank se poochta hai ki ye ID kiski hai
2️⃣ Aapka PIN encrypted jaata hai, payment app ko bhi nahi dikhta. Sirf aapka bank use check karta hai
3️⃣ Aapke bank se debit, chaiwale ke bank mein credit, dono bank NPCI ko "done" bolte hain
4️⃣ Paisa kata par pahuncha nahi? Payment fail maani jaati hai aur paisa apne aap wapas aata hai

🎯 Interview mein bolo: "UPI ID se NPCI sahi bank dhoondhta hai, PIN sirf remitter bank verify karta hai, phir debit-credit aur NPCI switch dono ko jodta hai."

📊 Sirf August 2026 mein 2,451 crore UPI transactions (NPCI data).
