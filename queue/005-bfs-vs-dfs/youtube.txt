Ek hi maze pe BFS aur DFS ki race.

BFS (Breadth First Search): level by level, queue se. DFS (Depth First Search): ek raasta end tak, stack se.
DFS ne 88 cells dekhe, BFS ne 213. Par raasta: DFS 78 steps, BFS sirf 28.
Kyun? BFS ne 27 step tak ke saare cells pehle hi dekh liye, exit nahi mila, toh 28 hi shortest hai (jab har step barabar ho).
Code mein fark sirf ek line: q.popleft() vs st.pop().

Maze code se generate hua hai; dono algorithms asli mein chale, numbers real hain.
