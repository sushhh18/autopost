Sirf kuch servers band... aur Microsoft DOWN! 🁢 Part 2: Domino effect 👇

1️⃣ Traffic normal tha, bas kuch servers achanak band ho gaye
2️⃣ Unka load baaki servers pe aaya, wo bhi overload hone lage
3️⃣ Toll plaza socho: 10 mein se 3 booth band, line lambi, baaki bhi jaam
4️⃣ Health check (servers ko check karne wala system) bhi fail, theek server bhi "beemar" dikhe
5️⃣ Servers wapas chalu kiye, traffic aate hi phir band
6️⃣ Fix: traffic thoda-thoda andar, naye updates pause

💡 Isse kehte hain cascading failure: ek failure doosre ko bhi down kar deta hai.
