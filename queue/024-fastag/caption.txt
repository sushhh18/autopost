FASTag kaise kaam karta hai:
1. Pehchaan: windshield sticker mein passive RFID chip + antenna hai, koi battery nahi. Reader ki radio waves ki energy se chip jaagti hai aur apni ID bhej deti hai.
2. Permission: toll plaza ke paas pehle se ek exception list hoti hai (blacklisted / low balance tags). Tumhari ID list mein nahi → barrier khul jaata hai. Bank se abhi kuch poocha hi nahi gaya!
3. Payment baad mein: toll plaza → toll ka bank (acquirer) → NPCI ka NETC switch → tumhara bank (issuer) → debit + SMS. Usually 15 min ke andar online process hota hai, isliye SMS kabhi late aata hai.
4. Isko kehte hain async processing: jo zaruri hai abhi, baaki baad mein.
5. Risk: tag 60 min se zyada blacklisted / low balance aur read ke 10 min baad tak fix nahi → payment decline, cash mein double toll. Galat deduction pe chargeback, 15 din ke cooling period ke baad.

India mein roz ~1.1 crore FASTag transactions hote hain (Sept 2026: 33.7 crore transactions, ₹7,096 crore).

Honesty note: ₹95 sirf example toll hai, screen pe dikhaye tag IDs bhi example hain. Steps NPCI/NETC ke published guidelines pe based hain.
