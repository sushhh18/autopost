2020, Berlin: ek aadmi ne 99 phones ek thele mein daale aur Google Maps pe khaali sadak LAAL ho gayi 🤯 Maps ko jam ka pata kaise chalta hai? 👇

1️⃣ Jinhone location sharing ON rakhi hai, unke phones bina naam ke location aur speed bhejte hain
2️⃣ Har dot ek chalti gaadi. Dots tez = green, dheere = orange, ruke hue = laal
3️⃣ Thele ke 99 dheere phones = Google ko laga 99 gaadiyan phasi hain, isliye baaki logon ko doosra raasta mila
4️⃣ ETA ke liye purane patterns bhi (jaise har Monday 9 baje jam) + machine learning

🎯 Interview mein bolo: "Crowdsourced anonymous location data se road ki average speed, usse traffic colour. Live + historical data + ML se ETA."

📊 DeepMind ke saath kuch shehron mein ETA accuracy 50% tak behtar hui; Google ke mutabik 97%+ trips pe ETA sahi.
