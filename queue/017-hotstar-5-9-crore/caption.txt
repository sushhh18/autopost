5.9 crore log, ek match, ek saath live 🏏 Server gira kyun nahi? Interview ka classic sawaal, jawab yahan 👇

1️⃣ Cricket traffic dheere nahi, ek jhatke mein aata hai: bada wicket ya last over pe lakhon log ek saath app kholte hain
2️⃣ Auto-scaling spike ke liye slow hai, isliye match se PEHLE hi extra servers chalu (pre-scaling) aur nakli bheed se load test
3️⃣ Video CDN (Content Delivery Network) se aata hai, aapke shehar ke paas wale server se
4️⃣ Phir bhi overload? Kam zaroori features dheere (scorecard kam refresh), apps ko "thoda ruko" signal, par video kabhi band nahi

🎯 Interview mein bolo: "Spike se pehle pre-scale + load testing, video CDN se, aur overload pe graceful degradation + client back-off."
