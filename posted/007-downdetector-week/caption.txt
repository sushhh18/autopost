Ek hafte mein 1.4 crore outage complaints! Outlook down, America ki saari flights ruki, bank apps band. Aur sabse pehle pakda Downdetector ne. Par kaise? 👇

1️⃣ Downdetector khud servers check nahi karta, logon ki complaints ginta hai
2️⃣ Jaise poori gali ki light jaaye to power cut, ek ghar ki jaaye to fuse
3️⃣ Har app ka ek normal level (baseline) hota hai
4️⃣ Complaints baseline se kai guna = outage ka alarm
5️⃣ Is hafte: Chase bank pe normal se 165 guna, Microsoft pe 78 lakh reports
6️⃣ Ek din mein 67 services flag, aam din mein sirf 16
7️⃣ Catch: complaint ≠ confirmed failure. Isliye companies health checks aur status page rakhti hain
8️⃣ Downdetector ke expert: sab ek ittefaq tha, koi ek hack nahi

ℹ️ Numbers Downdetector (Ookla) ke data se, Fortune aur NBC News ki reports mein. Downdetector ka exact algorithm public nahi; baseline aur spike general concept hai.
